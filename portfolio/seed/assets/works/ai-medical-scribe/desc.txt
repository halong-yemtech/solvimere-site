Title: AI Medical Scribe for Clinicians: Automating Patient Documentation

Description:
AI Apps & Integration
Python , Machine Learning , PyTorch , NLP Tokenization , Natural Language Processing , LLM Prompt , Large Language Model , Figma , Node.js , React , AWS Application
I worked with Heidi Health to develop an AI-powered medical scribe designed to assist clinicians by automating the process of documenting patient encounters. This AI tool listens to doctor-patient conversations in real time and generates accurate medical notes, enabling clinicians to focus more on patient care rather than administrative tasks.

The AI scribe uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) and speech recognition to transcribe and understand medical conversations, ensuring that the generated notes are both accurate and compliant with medical standards.

Key Technologies Used:

- Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning: To power the scribe's decision-making and note generation.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP): For understanding and processing medical terminology and context.
- Speech Recognition: To accurately transcribe doctor-patient conversations in real time.
- Cloud Computing: For scalable deployment and integration with electronic health records (EHR) systems.
- Python & TensorFlow: Used for AI model development and machine learning algorithm implementation.

My Role:
I was involved in the development and integration of the AI algorithms for transcribing medical conversations and creating accurate medical records. My tasks included:

Developing and optimizing NLP models for accurate medical note generation.
Integrating speech recognition for real-time transcription of doctor-patient conversations.
Ensuring the system’s seamless integration with cloud infrastructure for scalability and security.
